About

From criminology classrooms in Dhaka to Learning Centres in the world's largest refugee settlement

I was born on the coast of Cox's Bazar and returned home to work in the humanitarian response — bringing research training from the University of Dhaka into education, protection, gender and livelihood programmes. My work sits at the meeting point of numbers and narratives: databases that stand up to a Data Quality Assessment, and stories that honour the dignity of the people behind the data.

Whether it is a 20,000-record survey dataset, a donor report, a storytelling book preserving Rohingya heritage, or a feedback mechanism that actually closes the loop with communities — the goal is the same: evidence that improves programmes for the people they serve.
