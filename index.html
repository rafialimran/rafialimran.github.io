About

I have nearly four years of combined MEAL and research experience in Cox's Bazar, across IRC-funded education programming and Oxfam-funded, DFAT-supported gender, protection and livelihoods work.

My work covers field monitoring, baseline, midline and endline surveys, on-distribution and post-distribution monitoring, price monitoring, beneficiary selection, indicator tracking, data quality checks, beneficiary databases and multi-grant reporting.

I hold BSS and MSS degrees in Criminology from the University of Dhaka. I care about ethical data, community accountability and steady improvement in programme quality.
